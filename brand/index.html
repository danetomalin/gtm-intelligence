What customers are saying

Feedback themes (6)

Subscription Lock-In and Cancellation Friction

criticalOnboarding/Support Experiencefreq: high

Customers across multiple segments (general wellness, weight loss, men's health, convenience-oriented professionals) report significant friction when attempting to cancel subscriptions. Complaints center on aggressive retention tactics, unclear cancellation processes, difficulty reaching customer support, and perceived lock-in to costly plans. This pattern appears consistently in personas for value-conscious users and convenience-oriented professionals, suggesting a systemic customer experience issue that is damaging trust and driving churn.

Recommended action
Investigate and communicate. Conduct immediate audit of cancellation workflows and customer support responsiveness. Implement transparent, friction-free cancellation process and publish clear communication about no-lock-in policy. This is a trust and retention issue that directly contradicts stated convenience positioning.

Billing Transparency and Unexpected Charges

criticalPricing/Packaging Feedbackfreq: high

Customers report unexpected charges, unclear subscription terms, and lack of transparency in billing and renewal processes. This issue spans value-conscious users, convenience-oriented professionals, and general wellness seekers. Customers express feeling exploited by complex billing structures and difficulty understanding the true cost of service. The pattern suggests a gap between stated transparent pricing positioning and actual customer experience, particularly around medication costs separate from membership fees and unclear renewal terms.

Recommended action
Build and communicate. Redesign billing and pricing communication to clearly separate membership fees from medication costs. Implement upfront cost disclosure at point of purchase. Create transparent renewal notifications with opt-in (not opt-out) model. Publish clear pricing pages and FAQs. This directly addresses a core objection and competitive vulnerability vs. Sesame Care's transparent pricing positioning.

Limited Customer Support Channels and Responsiveness

highOnboarding/Support Experiencefreq: high

Multiple personas report that customer support is limited to messaging only, with no phone or email support available. This is particularly problematic for mental health patients who may need urgent support, and for customers with billing issues or cancellation requests. Customers report difficulty reaching support, unresolved billing disputes, and poor responsiveness. The gap is especially acute for mental health services where synchronous support may be clinically important for patients in crisis.

Recommended action
Build. Implement phone and email support channels, particularly for mental health patients and billing/cancellation requests. Establish SLA for response times. This is a critical gap for a healthcare platform and directly impacts patient safety (mental health) and customer satisfaction. Competitive differentiation opportunity vs. messaging-only competitors.
